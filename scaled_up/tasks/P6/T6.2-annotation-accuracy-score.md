Notable misses (generalizable limits of trace-only annotation):
1. Slot-FIELD semantics — 0x2B3D SET_VELOCITY read as SET_ANIM_FRAME (2 MISSes).
2. Entity identity from attract-only — credits-vs-score (0x18E0/0x18CD/0x1908),
   player-vs-robot (0x1F91). Attract never enters credited play.
3. Same draw mechanism, different intent — 0x25CA/0x25D4/0x264C/0x2662 are PLAYER
   LIFE ICONS, labelled DRAW_MAZE_* (identical magic-RAM blit shape as walls).
4. Trigger context — 0x2BE4 random speech right, "on leaving room" trigger missed.

Recovered well: bolt engine, magic-RAM draw pipeline, RANDOM (exact), interrupt
structure, sound-effect triggers, job creation — including cryptic canonicals
(C.LOAD/RTOAX/SR.TAB/LTABLE/CLEAR_CHYRON/SHOWO) where the descriptive name beat
the label.

HUMAN-GATE: the HIT/PARTIAL/MISS verdicts are semantic judgement calls. Sudnya
ratifies the scoring (or adjusts borderline calls) before this flips to done.
The cluster analysis (not the single %) is the durable result.
