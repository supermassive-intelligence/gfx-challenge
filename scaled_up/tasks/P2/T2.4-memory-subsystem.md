### Fill values -- MAME-verified (resolved)
Sudnya ran the debugger (berzerk loaded): `print b@3800` = 0xFF, `print b@c000`
= 0x00. These are TWO mechanisms, not one: 0x3800-0x3FFF is the empty ROM6
socket (mapped ROM, unloaded -> ROM_UNLOADED_FILL 0xFF); 0xC000-0xFFFF is
noprw() truly-unmapped -> UNMAPPED_FILL 0x00. The session's provisional single
0xFF was right for 0x3800 but would have been wrong for 0xC000 -- verifying
caught a real oracle divergence. Both constants are pinned in
machine/src/memory.js + tests; §2 of the contract distinguishes the rows;
decisions.md records the resolution.
