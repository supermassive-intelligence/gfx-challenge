## Context
The heart of Berzerk's rendering AND collision detection. Writes through the
magicram window pass through a hardware shifter/flipper (74181 ALU ops)
before landing in VRAM; the intercept flag latches collisions for the CPU to
read back. Spec: `cdoc/hardware-berzerk.md` §4 (video) and §3 (intercept read
port). Get this wrong and gameplay is subtly broken everywhere.
